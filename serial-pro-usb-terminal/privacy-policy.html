Privacy Policy - Serial Pro USB Terminal

Effective date: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Serial Pro USB Terminal mobile application developed and provided by Daniel Ndifon (the "Service Provider").

Information Collection and Use

Serial Pro USB Terminal does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information about its users.

The Application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use its functionality.

The Application does not collect or transmit information such as:

  • Your name, email address, or other contact information
  • Your device's IP address
  • Application usage or analytics data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Location information
  • Browsing activity
  • Serial communication data
  • Commands entered into the terminal
  • Files created or exported by the Application

USB Device Access

Serial Pro USB Terminal requires access to compatible USB devices in order to provide its primary serial communication functionality.

Information obtained from a connected USB device, such as device identification and serial communication data, is processed locally on the user's device as necessary to provide the Application's functionality.

The Service Provider does not receive or remotely store this information.

Local Data and Exported Files

Serial communication data and files that the user chooses to save or export are handled locally on the user's device.

The Application does not automatically upload serial data, terminal contents, commands, or exported files to the Service Provider or to external servers.

Users are responsible for managing files they choose to save, export, copy, or share using their device.

Internet and Network Communication

Serial Pro USB Terminal does not use an Internet connection to transmit user data to the Service Provider.

The Application does not operate a remote server or cloud service for collecting user information.

Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

Serial Pro USB Terminal does not use advertising services, analytics services, or tracking technologies to monitor users.

The Application does not use cookies, advertising trackers, analytics trackers, or similar technologies for collecting information about users.

Third-Party Services

The Application does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services that collect personal information through the Application.

Distribution of the Application through Google Play is subject to Google's own terms and privacy practices. Google's handling of information associated with Google Play is separate from the data practices of Serial Pro USB Terminal.

Data Sharing

The Service Provider does not sell, rent, trade, or share users' personal information because the Application does not collect such information.

Data Retention and Deletion

The Service Provider does not maintain a database of personal information collected through Serial Pro USB Terminal.

Because personal information is not collected or stored by the Service Provider through the Application, there is no personal information held by the Service Provider that requires deletion when the Application is uninstalled.

Data or files intentionally saved by the user on their device remain under the user's control and can be deleted using the appropriate device or file-management functions.

Children's Privacy

Serial Pro USB Terminal is a technical utility and is not specifically directed toward children.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Application. Since the Application does not collect personal information from its users, it does not knowingly maintain personal information belonging to children.

Security

The Application is designed so that its serial communication functionality and associated data processing occur locally on the user's device.

Users should exercise appropriate care when connecting USB hardware and when saving, exporting, copying, or sharing serial data that may itself contain confidential or sensitive information.

International Data Transfers

The Service Provider does not transfer personal information collected through Serial Pro USB Terminal internationally because the Application does not collect or transmit such information to the Service Provider.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Application's functionality or data practices change.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
